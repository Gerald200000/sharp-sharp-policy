Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026
In short: Sharp Sharp has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email or other personal details. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

Who we are

Sharp Sharp (the “app”) is a brain-teaser puzzle game for Android published by PureForm Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information. The app does not use your camera or microphone. We do not run our own servers for the app, so we never receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your place in the game, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • which levels you have solved, the level you are on, and your coins;
  • which hints you have unlocked for each level;
  • whether you have already claimed the one-time free-coins video;
  • whether the app has already asked you to rate it at certain levels;
  • your settings, such as music and sound on or off.

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it permanently.

Motion sensor

A few puzzles ask you to tilt or shake your phone. To do this, the app reads your device’s motion sensor (accelerometer) while you play. These readings are used instantly on your device to move things in the puzzle. They are never stored and never sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. It shows a banner ad while you play a puzzle, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos that you choose to watch for a hint, to see an answer, to double your coins, or for free coins. To serve and measure these ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as your country);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads are shown and whether they are tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad loading errors.

Google handles this information under its own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

We ask Google to limit ads in the app to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Ratings and reviews (Google Play)

After you reach certain levels, the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window, and the star button on the home screen opens the app’s page on Google Play. These are handled entirely by Google Play. If you leave a rating or review, it is posted under your Google account according to Google Play’s policies. We do not receive any information from this window, including whether you rated the app.

Your consent and choices

  • Consent where required. If you are in a region where the law requires consent for personalised ads (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), the app asks for your choice the first time you open it, using Google’s consent tool. Where that applies, you can change your choice at any time with the privacy (shield) button on the app’s home screen.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it to opt out of personalised ads, in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the wording varies by device).
  • Reward videos are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

Age requirement

Sharp Sharp is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or US state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal information, such as the right to access, correct or delete it, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold your personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled through the choices above or directly with Google. You are still welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

Security

Your game data stays on your device. Information collected for ads is sent to Google over encrypted connections and is protected under Google’s security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will note it in the app’s update notes on Google Play.
